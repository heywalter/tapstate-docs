Compare the Cloud plans below, then create a Cluster through checkout. After
purchase, use **Plans & billing** in the organization console to manage the
subscription. Each Cluster has a separate subscription.

For database ownership, access controls, backups, and data handling, see
[Data security and responsibilities](/docs/manage/data-responsibilities).

## Plans and trial

Each Cluster has its own subscription. Monthly prices below are in USD and
exclude applicable taxes:

| Plan | vCPU | RAM | Managed storage | Monthly price | Trial |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2C8G Starter | 2 | 8 GB | 10 GB | $199 | 7 days |
| 4C16G Pro | 4 | 16 GB | 20 GB | $399 | No trial |
| 8C32G Enterprise | 8 | 32 GB | 50 GB | $799 | No trial |

Managed storage is the capacity listed on the Cluster creation page. It is
separate from the Atlas databases you provide during storage setup and does not
include capacity or credits in your own Atlas account. Account for
[databases you manage separately](/docs/manage/data-responsibilities#identify-who-manages-each-resource)
when estimating pipeline costs—the tapstate trial does not waive charges from
those providers.

- **7-day Starter trial:** Each organization is eligible for one 7-day
  **2C8G Starter** trial and can start without a payment card. Automatic
  renewal authorization is still required at checkout. If the organization
  already has a trial or active subscription, choose **4C16G Pro** or
  **8C32G Enterprise** for additional Clusters or [contact support](#get-help).
- **Trial checkout and conversion:** Complete Stripe checkout even when no card
  is required (**Continue to checkout** does not impose an immediate charge for
  a cardless Starter trial). Adding a valid payment method with auto-renew
  enabled converts the same Cluster to a paid monthly subscription at the end of
  the 7 days; without a payment method (or if renewal is canceled during the
  trial), the trial ends on its scheduled date and the platform releases the
  Cluster resources.
- **No paid-plan trial:** **4C16G Pro** and **8C32G Enterprise** require the
  first monthly payment at checkout. Review the selected plan, currency, trial
  terms, recurring charge, and total shown at checkout before subscribing.

<Aside type="note">
  A Cluster's provisioning completion time does not extend or reset the billing
  period. If signup and Billing show conflicting dates, see
  [Trial dates do not match](#trial-dates-do-not-match).
</Aside>

## Subscribe and manage billing

1. Sign in to [tapstate Cloud](https://cloud.tapstate.com/), confirm the
   active organization in the sidebar, and select **Provision Cluster** on
   **Overview**.
2. Enter a Cluster name, pick a region (cannot be changed later), select a plan,
   review pricing and renewal authorization, and select **Continue to
   checkout**.
3. Complete Stripe checkout, then return to setup and wait for **Cluster is
   ready** before selecting **Open Cluster Console** and finishing
   [storage setup](/docs/quickstart#2-connect-storage).
4. To check or manage an active subscription later, select the organization name
   above the Cluster to open the organization console, open **Plans & billing**,
   and select **Refresh** to inspect subscription status, **Auto-renew**,
   **Current period**, and the associated **Cluster**.

[![Plans and billing showing a Starter trial and the selected plan](/images/cloud/billing-plan.png)](/images/cloud/billing-plan.png)

Check **Clusters** separately for resource readiness before opening a
workspace; an active subscription or **Auto-renew** label alone does not confirm
that provisioning succeeded or that a trial has a payment method on file.
In-place plan upgrades and downgrades are not currently available—[contact
support](#get-help) before canceling or creating a second subscription, as a new
subscription creates a separate Cluster lifecycle without migrating pipelines or
data.

## Cancel a subscription

Cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current trial or paid period; the
Cluster remains usable until that date. At period end, the platform terminates
the Cluster and reclaims platform-provided databases (such as the
platform-managed Configuration database in a three-layer installation). Your
own Atlas cache and application databases remain your responsibility—save or
export any required configuration and data beforehand (see
[retention and cancellation](/docs/manage/data-responsibilities#plan-for-retention-and-cancellation)).

1. Open **Plans & billing** in the intended organization.
2. Verify the associated **Cluster** and **Current period**.
3. Select **Cancel subscription**, review the confirmation, and confirm
   **Yes, cancel** only if you want to stop renewal.
4. Refresh Billing and confirm **Pending cancellation**, the expected period end,
   and that automatic renewal is off.

Stopping a pipeline does not cancel a subscription, and cancellation does not
automatically delete or back up customer-managed Atlas databases.

**Refund policy:** Tapstate does not offer voluntary or prorated refunds for
unused time, including mid-period cancellation or a renewal not canceled in
time (refunds required by applicable law remain available; the free trial has no
subscription charge). See the
[Subscription & Cancellation Policy, section 6](https://tapstate.com/legal/subscription-policy)
and [Terms of Service](https://tapstate.com/legal/eula), or
[contact support](#get-help) with your subscription ID, amount, and charge date
for a disputed charge. A support request alone does not cancel renewal.

## Billing FAQ

<Accordions>
<Accordion id="checkout-did-not-complete" title="Checkout timed out or did not complete—should I try again?">

No—do not start another checkout immediately. Return to the organization
console and refresh **Plans & billing** first to see whether the attempt went
through. For a declined payment, follow the instructions from Stripe or your
card issuer; an interrupted browser tab does not prove payment failed. If the
status stays unclear, [contact support](#get-help) with the visible status and
attempt timestamp.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="bank-authentication" title="Why is my bank asking for 3D Secure (3DS) or extra verification?">

Stripe may require bank authentication such as 3DS before authorizing renewal
or charging a card. Complete the prompt in the payment flow, then return to
**Plans & billing** and refresh. Completing bank verification alone does not
confirm the subscription or Cluster is ready until provisioning finishes.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="duplicate-checkout" title="What if checkout was submitted more than once?">

Refresh **Plans & billing** in the organization console and check both the
subscriptions and associated Clusters before retrying—do not create another
organization or subscription to work around an uncertain payment state. If you
see duplicate subscriptions or unexpected charges, report all affected
subscription IDs, amounts, and attempt timestamps to [support](#get-help).

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="missing-plan-or-price" title="Why are my plan or price details missing in Billing?">

Confirm that you are viewing the intended organization in **Plans & billing**
and select **Refresh**. A missing display does not mean a zero charge, and the
public pricing table above does not override an existing subscription's terms.
Use your Stripe checkout confirmation email when asking [support](#get-help) to
reconcile the subscription rather than creating a replacement subscription.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="trial-dates-do-not-match" title="Why do signup and Billing show different trial dates?">

Starter's free trial always runs for 7 days. Do not infer an extended trial
from a conflicting date during setup; include both displayed dates and your
subscription ID when asking [support](#get-help) to confirm the true period end.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="payment-completed-but-the-cluster-is-not-ready" title="Payment completed, but my Cluster is not ready—what should I do?">

On the setup page, **Waiting for Stripe to confirm the payment…** means
confirmation is still pending; keep the page open or select **Retry** if it
errors rather than starting a new checkout. If provisioning stops with
**Provisioning interrupted**, inspect the failed stage and use **Retry
Provisioning** when offered. If you closed the tab, check **Clusters** and
**Plans & billing** for the existing subscription and report the Cluster name,
subscription ID, timestamp, and visible message to [support](#get-help) if the
error persists.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="cluster-still-opens-after-cancellation" title="I canceled my subscription, why does my Cluster still open?">

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current trial or paid billing
period, so the Cluster remains accessible until that date. Refresh **Plans &
billing** to verify **Pending cancellation** and the scheduled end date rather
than assuming the cancellation request failed.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Get help

Open **Support** in the organization console to create a ticket or review **My
tickets** (requires a verified account email; select **Refresh tickets** before
resubmitting if interrupted), or email
[support@tapstate.com](mailto:support@tapstate.com).

Include the organization, Cluster, and subscription IDs, the action taken, the
timestamp with time zone, and the visible status or error. Crop or redact
screenshots beforehand, and never send passwords, database connection URIs,
payment card numbers, or authentication codes.

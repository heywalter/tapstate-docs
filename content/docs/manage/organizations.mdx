An organization provides the context for your Cloud Clusters and subscription.
Check the selected organization before opening a Cluster or changing billing.

## Sign in

Open [tapstate Cloud](https://cloud.tapstate.com/) and sign in
with your account. If you received an invitation, use its link to complete the
account setup shown there. An invitation to join an existing organization and
access to create your own organization are different starting points.

If the page offers **Sign in with an email code**, you can use it for an
existing account: request the code, check the intended mailbox, and enter the
code on the sign-in page. An email verification code is different from the
trial code or invitation link used for account setup. If registration reports
that your email is already registered, return to sign-in instead of creating
another account.

If a link is expired, already used, or intended for another email address,
request a replacement from the person who invited you. Do not create an
additional organization merely to work around an invitation error.

## Check the selected organization

Check the organization name at the top of the sidebar. To review its details
and team, open **Settings** > **Organization & team**. The name in the sidebar
is a label, not an organization switcher. If the console opens the wrong
organization, contact [support](/docs/manage/billing#get-help) with the intended
organization name.

If the expected organization is missing, check that you signed in with the
invited account and ask its owner to confirm your access.

## Review account and team settings

In the organization console, open **Settings**. **Account** shows your profile
and linked sign-in methods. **Organization & team** shows the organization and
its invitations. These are Cloud account settings, separate from the Cluster's
storage and pipeline configuration.

[![Organization and team settings with the invitation entry point](/images/cloud/organization-settings.png)](/images/cloud/organization-settings.png)

To invite a member when your account offers this action, select **Create
invitation** under **Organization invitations** and share the resulting link
only with the intended recipient. The form also offers an invitee email field.
Treat the link as access to your organization: it can be used once before it
expires. Check the invitation's status and expiry in the list. An empty list
means no invitations are listed, not that the organization has no members.

Organization details marked **Changes to these settings are not available yet**
are read-only. Do not use another organization to work around a missing edit
control; contact support for the required change.

## Open a Cluster workspace

Open **Clusters**, find the intended Cluster, and open it when its status is
**Ready**. In the workspace, use **Data Sources**, **Pipelines**, and **Views**
to work with data. See [Clusters](/docs/manage/clusters) for network preparation
and setup requirements.

To return to organization management, select the organization name above the
Cluster in the workspace sidebar. **Plans & billing** belongs to the organization
console. To open Cluster settings, use the account menu at the bottom of the
workspace sidebar and select **Settings**.

## Review the subscription

Open **Plans & billing** in the organization console and verify the organization and
associated Cluster before taking action. If an action is unavailable to your
account, ask the organization owner to review it. See
[Pricing and billing](/docs/manage/billing).

## Open support

Open **Support** in the organization console to submit a ticket (**Create a
ticket**) or check existing requests (**My tickets**). Creating a ticket
requires a verified Cloud account email. If a submission result is uncertain,
select **Refresh tickets** in **My tickets** before submitting again to avoid
duplicates. See [Get help](/docs/manage/billing#get-help) for what to include.
